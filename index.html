Frequently asked questions

A Duda specialist is a designer-developer who builds exclusively on the Duda platform — the CMS used by thousands of agencies for client websites. Instead of learning the platform on your project, I bring 500+ shipped Duda builds, so your site launches faster, converts better, and hands off cleanly to your team or agency.

Yes — white-label Duda production is my core service. I build under your agency's brand with templated systems, widget libraries, and documented handoff, so your clients never know I exist. I currently partner with agencies across the US and Australia and work in their time zones.

Most standard business websites launch in 2–4 weeks from kickoff, including design, build, content placement, and SEO setup. Larger builds — multi-location, e-commerce, or aviation sites with fleet and booking structures — typically run 4–8 weeks. You get a realistic timeline before we start.

Every build ships SEO-ready: semantic heading structure, local business schema, service-area pages where relevant, optimized page speed and Core Web Vitals, mobile-first layouts, and clean URL architecture. For home services and local businesses, I structure sites specifically to win Google Maps and local search.

Absolutely. I take over existing Duda sites for redesigns, conversion improvements, speed fixes, and SEO restructuring. Because I work inside Duda daily, I can audit what's hurting your site and fix it without a from-scratch rebuild — unless one genuinely makes sense.
